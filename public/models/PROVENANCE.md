# MEDVERSE 3D — Medical 3D Model Provenance & Asset Pipeline

## Phase 01 Default Representation
The built-in cardiovascular 3D assembly is a **Schematic Educational Demonstration** mapped 1:1 to 10 verified cardiovascular anatomy records. It is explicitly labeled as a schematic diagram and is never presented as a clinically accurate anatomical mesh.

## Phase 02 Verified NIH 3D Target Assets
Download and place verified GLB/GLTF models into `public/models/` after confirming license attribution:
- NIH 3D Heart Library (MRI-derived models): https://3d.nih.gov/collections/heart-library
- Normal Human Heart (Diastole) — `3DPX-002636`: https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3DPX-002636
- Visible Human Heart Vessels and Lungs — `3DPX-023212`: https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3DPX-023212
- Heart with Ascending & Descending Aorta — `3DPX-000906`: https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3DPX-000906
